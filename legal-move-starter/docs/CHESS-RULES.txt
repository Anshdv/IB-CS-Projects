HOW THE PIECES MOVE
===================
You do not need to know chess to finish this assignment. Everything
you need is here. The board is empty except for the piece that is
moving, so you never have to worry about anything being in the way.

Squares are named by a file letter a-h and a rank number 1-8, like e4.
White pawns move toward rank 8. Black pawns move toward rank 1.

  KNIGHT   Two squares one way and one square the other, in an L.
           b1 to c3 is legal. b1 to d2 is also legal.
           e4 to g6 is NOT legal - that is two and two, not two and one.

  ROOK     Any distance along a rank or a file, but not both.
           a1 to a8 legal. a1 to h1 legal. a1 to b2 not legal.

  BISHOP   Any distance along a diagonal.
           c1 to h6 legal. c1 to c5 not legal.

  QUEEN    Anything a rook can do, or anything a bishop can do.

  KING     One square in any direction.

  PAWN     The complicated one. It moves and captures differently.
           Not capturing: one square straight forward. OR two squares
             straight forward, but only from its starting rank
             (rank 2 for white, rank 7 for black).
           Capturing: one square diagonally forward. Never straight.
           A pawn can never move backwards.

NO PIECE MAY "MOVE" TO THE SQUARE IT IS ALREADY ON.
